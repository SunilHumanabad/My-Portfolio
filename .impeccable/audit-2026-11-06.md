## Open findings
- **[P1] Reduced-motion override** — `site.motion.respectReducedMotion: false` means visitors with vestibular sensitivity get the auto-playing build. Mitigated by Skip, Esc and the remembered off switch (meets WCAG 2.2.2), not by default. Set it to `true` to honour the OS setting. Suggested: `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P2] Build hides content on load** — readers wait for cursors to reach a block (focus reveals instantly). Consider a shorter path for the hero only. Suggested: `/impeccable animate`.
- **[P3] Section and project numbers** (01–04, outlined 01–03) carry no information; candidates to remove. Suggested: `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P3] Live-build layout reads** — `getBoundingClientRect` on every pending block every 90 ms; fine at 117 blocks, revisit if the page grows. Suggested: `/impeccable optimize`.
- **[P3] Hard-coded colours** — move `#4fd27c` and button `#fff` into tokens. Suggested: `/impeccable polish`.
